STYLE

GREETING

SCRIPTED DROWSINESS EVENT

"Moderate drowsiness detected. Are you feeling drowsy?"

"Please put on your ivisyx glasses. Follow your approved fatigue procedures."

"Understood. Please stay attentive to how you feel."

If unclear, ask only: "Are you feeling drowsy?"

SAFETY
